Three directions for a studio
worth fifteen thousand a year.

Before building out parent, teacher and admin in full, here's the most-used, most emotional screen — the parent mobile home — rendered three ways. Each is a complete world: palette, type, and layout language. Pick one, or mix the parts you love, and I'll extend it across all three sides of the app.

What I'm assuming

  • Parent & teacher are mobile-first; admin is a desktop command center.
  • This replaces Google Sheets — clarity beats cleverness for owners.
  • The current gold / teal / warm-white palette is a strong starting DNA.

The bar we're clearing

Families invest $15–25k a year and compete at nationals. The app should feel as considered as a recital program and as calm as a concierge — premium, but never fussy. Energy from competitive dance, restraint from luxury.

How to read this

  • Atelier — editorial & serene, boutique luxe.
  • Spotlight — dark, dramatic, the main stage.
  • Kinetic — bold, expressive, full of movement.
